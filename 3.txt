### 1\. Understanding HELSB

HELSB stands for Higher Education Loans and Scholarships Board. It is a Zambian government institution established under the Higher Education Loans and Scholarships Act No. 31 of 2016. Its responsibility is to administer student loans and scholarships to eligible Zambian students pursuing higher education.

The purpose of HELSB is to help students who have financial difficulties access university education. It provides financial assistance to eligible students whose families may not have enough money to pay for university expenses. It is particularly important for students from financially disadvantaged households, orphans, students supported by vulnerable guardians, children of unemployed parents and students from families that depend on small-scale farming.

HELSB is not a university. It does not admit students into universities. Students must first obtain admission to an eligible institution before applying for a government student loan. Admission into a university does not automatically guarantee a student loan. Every application is assessed according to HELSB requirements, financial vulnerability and available funding. ### 3\. Who qualifies for HELSB student loans?

To qualify for consideration, an applicant must be a Zambian citizen with a valid Green National Registration Card. The applicant must have obtained admission to one of the ten supported institutions for the academic year stated in the HELSB advertisement. The applicant must also satisfy the applicable age and Grade 12 completion requirements.

For the 2026/2027 academic year, applicants were required to have completed Grade 12 in 2022, 2023, 2024 or 2025 and be 26 years old or younger by 31st October 2026. Applicants also needed to demonstrate genuine financial need and satisfy the other conditions in the advertisement. These requirements must not automatically be applied to future academic years because HELSB may change them. HELSB Zambia +1

ASK MR TEES must explain that being poor alone does not automatically guarantee a loan. The applicant must meet the official eligibility conditions and provide the required evidence.

### 4\. Financial vulnerability

Financial vulnerability means that a student comes from a household that experiences serious financial difficulties and may not be able to afford university education.

An extremely vulnerable student may come from a household without a reliable source of income. The family may depend on small-scale farming, casual work, occasional assistance from relatives or other limited sources of income.

An orphan may experience financial difficulties because they have lost one or both parents and depend on relatives or guardians. A student whose parents are unemployed may also experience difficulties because the household has no regular income. A student living with a guardian may be vulnerable if the guardian has limited income and supports many dependants.

Students with disabilities may also have additional educational and financial support needs.

ASK MR TEES must encourage students to explain their real family circumstances and provide supporting evidence. It must never encourage applicants to exaggerate or invent financial difficulties.

### 5\. HELSB student loan support levels

HELSB provides different levels of tuition-fee support according to the applicant's financial circumstances and the Board's available funding.

The 100% support level means full tuition-fee support. This level is strictly reserved for extremely vulnerable applicants under the published 2026/2027 arrangements.

The 75% support level means HELSB covers 75% of the applicable tuition fees. The 50% support level means HELSB covers half of the applicable tuition fees. The 25% support level means HELSB covers one-quarter of the applicable tuition fees.

Applicants must indicate the support level they are requesting and provide evidence of their financial circumstances. However, HELSB makes the final decision. Students should not assume that requesting 100% support means they will automatically receive it.

The published 2026/2027 arrangements provide full student allowances regardless of the percentage of tuition-fee support awarded. This means that the percentage applies to tuition-fee support, not automatically to the student's allowances. HELSB Zambia +1

### 6\. What does the HELSB student loan cover?

HELSB student loans provide financial assistance towards tuition fees, registration fees and accommodation fees. They also provide student allowances for meals, books and academic projects according to the applicable sponsorship arrangements.

Tuition fees are payments required for academic programmes. Registration fees are payments required to register at the university. Accommodation support helps students meet approved accommodation expenses. Meal allowances help students buy food, while book allowances assist with learning materials. Project allowances help with approved academic project expenses.

The loan scheme also includes insurance arrangements for specified circumstances, including death and other covered events. Students must consult the official loan terms to understand the applicable coverage and conditions. HELSB Zambia +1

### 7\. Required HELSB application documents

ASK MR TEES must explain the purpose of every document because students need to understand why HELSB requests it.

The applicant's Green NRC is important because it identifies the student and proves Zambian citizenship. The parent's or guardian's NRC helps identify the person supporting the applicant and provides information about the student's family circumstances.

The Grade 12 Statement of Results or School Certificate provides evidence of the applicant's academic background. The university admission letter proves that the student has been admitted to an eligible institution and identifies the programme and academic year.

The student's application letter is a formal request for financial assistance. It should explain who the applicant is, which university admitted them, the programme they intend to study and why they require financial support.

A guardian's recommendation letter explains the guardian's relationship with the student and the household's financial circumstances. A school recommendation letter may provide additional information about the applicant's background. Church or community leader recommendation letters may also provide supporting information when requested or permitted.

A passport-size photograph helps with identification and administrative records where required. A disability card provides supporting evidence for students with disabilities where applicable.

Death certificates are important for applicants who have lost parents because they provide official evidence of the death and help explain the student's family circumstances.

An affidavit of means provides a formal explanation of the applicant's financial circumstances. It can support claims concerning poverty, unemployment, orphanhood, dependence on farming or financial difficulties experienced by the guardian.

The official 2026/2027 advertisement listed the application letter, admission letter, Grade 12 results or certificate, applicant's NRC and parent's or guardian's NRC as its main application documents. Other supporting documents must be prepared according to the latest HELSB advertisement and application guidance. HELSB Zambia +1

### 8\. Understanding affidavits

An affidavit is a written statement in which a person formally declares that the information provided is true. It is sworn or affirmed before an authorised person, such as a Commissioner for Oaths.

An affidavit of means explains the financial circumstances of a student and their household. It helps HELSB understand why the applicant requires financial assistance.

For example, a student whose parents are peasant farmers can explain that the family depends on seasonal farming and does not have a reliable monthly income. A student whose parents are unemployed can explain how the household survives without regular income. An orphan can explain who currently supports them, where they live and how they manage their expenses.

A student living with a guardian should explain the relationship with the guardian, the guardian's source of income and the number of dependants supported by the household.

An affidavit is important because it provides a formal explanation of financial circumstances that may not be clear from academic results or identity documents. It can strengthen an application when accompanied by genuine supporting documents.

ASK MR TEES may assist with drafting affidavits, but it must remind students that the information must be truthful and that the affidavit must be properly sworn or affirmed before an authorised person. An ordinary unsigned letter is not the same as a sworn affidavit.

### 9\. How to apply for a HELSB student loan

Students must first obtain admission to one of the ten supported institutions. After receiving an admission letter, they should check the latest HELSB advertisement to understand the eligibility conditions, application deadlines and required documents.

Students should prepare their documents carefully and visit the official application portal at [https://portal.helsb.gov.zm/](https://portal.helsb.gov.zm/?utm_source=chatgpt.com). They should follow the account creation instructions and provide correct personal details.

Applicants must complete the online application, select the correct university and programme, indicate their requested support level and provide the required financial information. They must upload their documents according to the portal instructions.

For the 2026/2027 advertisement, documents were required in PDF format, with a maximum file size of 5 MB per document. Students should review all information before submitting the application and keep a record of the submission.

Students are encouraged to apply early rather than waiting until the last day. Missing documents, incorrect information, internet problems and technical challenges can affect the application process. HELSB Zambia +1

### 10\. HELSB screening

Screening is the process of checking student loan applications and supporting documents to determine whether applicants meet the required conditions.

During screening, HELSB may examine citizenship, age, academic history, admission details, identity documents and evidence of financial need. An application may experience problems if documents are missing, unclear, incorrect or inconsistent.

Students should regularly check their application accounts and respond to official requests for additional information or corrections.

ASK MR TEES must explain that screening does not automatically mean a student has been awarded a loan. It is part of the application assessment process.

### 11\. HELSB appeals

An appeal is a formal request asking HELSB to reconsider an application decision or outcome.

A student who has not been selected should first understand the reason for the unsuccessful outcome. They should check whether the problem concerns missing documents, incorrect information, eligibility or insufficient supporting evidence.

The student should then follow the official appeal announcement and prepare the required supporting documents. An appeal letter should clearly explain the student's circumstances, the reason for requesting reconsideration and any additional evidence.

ASK MR TEES can help students understand the appeal process and prepare appeal letters. However, it must never promise that an appeal will be successful because the final decision belongs to HELSB.

### 12\. HELSB loan repayment

A student loan is financial assistance that must be repaid according to the applicable government loan terms. It is different from a non-repayable scholarship.

HELSB requires beneficiaries to begin repayment after graduation. Its published information provides a one-year grace period after graduation. Beneficiaries who settle their full outstanding balance within this period will not incur the standard 10% interest charge described in the published repayment information.

Students should understand their responsibilities before accepting a loan and should keep their loan records for future reference. HELSB Zambia
