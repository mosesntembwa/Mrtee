1. INTRODUCTION TO KAPASA MAKASA UNIVERSITY

Kapasa Makasa University (KMU) is a public university in Zambia, located in Chinsali District, Muchinga Province. The university is situated along the Great North Road at Mulakupikwa Village, north of Chinsali town.

The university's motto is "LEARN TO INNOVATE." Its purpose is to provide higher education, develop skills, encourage research and innovation, and prepare students to contribute to national development.

KMU offers programmes in areas such as Information and Communication Technology, Agriculture, Aquatic Sciences, Fisheries, Animal Science and other related fields.

Official website: https://www.kmu.ac.zm/

2. HISTORY OF KAPASA MAKASA UNIVERSITY

The facility that became Kapasa Makasa University was initially intended to be a police training institution known as Chinsali Combined Staff Training College. It was under the Ministry of Home Affairs during Zambia's First Republic.

On 29 July 1996, Cabinet transferred the facility from the Ministry of Home Affairs to the Ministry of Education or another appropriate ministry. A committee chaired by Professor Geoffrey Lungwangwa was later appointed to recommend how the facility could be developed.

The committee recommended that the institution should become a centre of excellence in university education, particularly in science and technology.

Today, KMU is an institution focused on higher education, innovation, research and skills development.

7. HOW TO APPLY TO KMU ONLINE

Applicants should use the official KMU application portal:
https://apanel.kmu.ac.zm/start

The general application process is as follows:

1. Open the official application portal.
2. Select the appropriate study category, such as undergraduate, degree, diploma, short course or another available category.
3. Enter a valid email address.
4. Check the email account for a verification code and use the code to verify the email address.
5. Select the programme you want to study.
6. Enter your personal information and next-of-kin details.
7. Enter your Grade 12 or other relevant academic results.
8. Upload the required documents, such as your NRC and academic results, together with any other documents requested by the portal.
9. Carefully review all the information before submitting the application.

After a successful application, an applicant number is generated. Applicants should save this number because it is important for payment and application follow-up.

The university's published application instructions list an application fee of K150. Applicants should confirm the amount displayed on the current portal before making payment because fees can change.

Where payment is required, follow the official instructions provided by the application system. The university's published guidance refers to payment through mobile money and designated bank branches, including Zanaco and Indo Zambia Bank.

Always use the correct applicant number and retain proof of payment.
